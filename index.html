NoSmoke AI Final Year Project

An AI-based system that detects cigarette smoking in live camera video, identifies the person against a student database, and automates alerts and records through a web dashboard.

Problem: Campus smoking bans rely on manual monitoring, which is inconsistent and can't cover indoor spaces.

My role: Team project (3 members). I contributed to the thesis research and developed the frontend of the web dashboard (React and Tailwind CSS).

ReactTailwind CSSWebSocketFastAPIYOLOv8sMTCNN + FaceNetMongoDB

Dashboard I built

  • Live detection view with confidence scores
  • Detection history with filters and CSV export
  • Student database and challan (fine) tracking screens
  • Admin-only login

System overview (team project)

  • YOLOv8s detects cigarettes; on detection an evidence frame is saved and MTCNN + FaceNet match the face against a student database
  • Matches are logged, and the student is emailed with a challan PDF; unmatched people are logged as "Unknown"
  • About 90% accuracy on a 352-image held-out test set, trained on 4,640 annotated images that include pens and straws as hard negatives
  • The team rejected a dataset scoring around 80-85% because its close-up images didn't match real CCTV footage, documented in our thesis

Tested with webcams, not a deployed campus CCTV network. Access is limited to admins, and privacy is documented as future-work priority.
